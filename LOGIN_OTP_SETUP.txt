TradeBook Login OTP

Login flow:
Email + Password -> Firebase password check -> Login OTP -> Verify OTP -> Dashboard

Worker endpoint:
POST /send-otp with {to, otp, type:"login"}
POST /verify-otp with {email, otp, type:"login"}

Brevo email subject:
TradeBook Login OTP

OTP expiry:
2 minutes (Cloudflare KV OTP_STORE)

Important:
Do not change FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID, FIREBASE_CLIENT_EMAIL, FIREBASE_PRIVATE_KEY,
BREVO_API_KEY, or OTP_STORE binding.
